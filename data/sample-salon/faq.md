# Maple Street Hair Studio — Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you sell gift cards?
Yes. Gift cards are available in the salon in any amount and never expire. They can be used for any service or product.

## How do I pay?
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Tips can be added to a card payment.

## Which colour brand do you use?
We use vegan, cruelty-free professional colour, and ammonia-free options are available on request.

## Do you work with curly hair?
Yes. Priya and Marcus specialise in curly and textured hair, and cut curls dry so you can see the shape as it's cut. Please come with your curls in their natural state, without a ponytail.

## How long does balayage last?
Balayage grows out softly, so most clients come back every three to four months. A toner and gloss in between keeps the colour fresh.

## Do I need a patch test?
Yes, if it's your first colour with us or you have reacted to hair dye before. See "Patch test before colour" in our services guide.

## Can I bring my child?
Yes. Children's cuts are for children under 12, and a parent or guardian stays in the salon during the appointment.

## Do you take walk-ins?
Walk-ins are welcome for men's cuts and fringe trims when a stylist is free. For everything else, please send an appointment request.
