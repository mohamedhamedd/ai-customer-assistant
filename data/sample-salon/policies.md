# Maple Street Hair Studio — Salon Policies

## Appointment requests
Requests made online or through our assistant are not confirmed appointments. Our front desk confirms each request by phone or text within one working day, and suggests another time if the stylist isn't free.

## Cancellations and late arrivals
Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or change an appointment. A cancellation with less notice, or a missed appointment, has a $25 fee.

If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your service or move it to another time, so the next client isn't kept waiting.

## Deposits
Balayage, keratin treatments and wedding day styling need a 30% deposit when the appointment is confirmed. The deposit is taken off the final bill, and refunded if you cancel with at least 48 hours' notice.

## If you're not happy
If you're not happy with your cut or colour, let us know within 7 days and we'll adjust it for free.

## Children
Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult during their appointment.
