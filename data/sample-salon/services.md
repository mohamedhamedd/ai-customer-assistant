# Maple Street Hair Studio — Services

Maple Street Hair Studio is an independent hair salon in Asheville, North Carolina. Our six stylists cut, colour and style all hair types, and two of them, Priya and Marcus, specialise in curly and textured hair.

## Cuts and styling

### Women's cut and style
A wash, a consultation about the look you want, the cut, and a blow-dry and style to finish. Bring photos if you have a style in mind.

### Men's cut
A clipper or scissor cut with a wash and a quick style. Beard tidy-ups can be added at no extra time.

### Children's cut
For children under 12. A parent or guardian stays in the salon during the appointment. We keep it quick and friendly, with a booster seat for little ones.

### Blow-dry and style
A wash and a blow-dry, smooth, voluminous or curled. Popular before an event or a night out.

### Fringe trim
A quick tidy of a fringe (bangs) between cuts. Walk-in only: just come by when it suits you.

## Colour

### Patch test before colour
Anyone having colour with us for the first time, or who has had a reaction to hair dye before, needs a patch test at least 48 hours before the colour appointment. It takes five minutes: a small dab of colour behind the ear. You can drop in for it any time we are open.

### Root touch-up
Colour on the regrowth only, to match your existing colour. Best every four to six weeks.

### Full colour
One colour from roots to ends, permanent or semi-permanent.

### Highlights
Foils that lighten selected strands. Partial highlights cover the top and the parting; full highlights cover the whole head.

### Balayage
Colour painted on by hand for a soft, natural, sun-kissed look that grows out without a hard line. Touch-ups are needed less often than with highlights, usually every three to four months.

### Toner and gloss
A short service after lightening, or on its own, to adjust the tone (less brassy, more ash or warmer) and add shine.

### Colour consultation
A free, short chat with a colourist before your first colour service: what you would like, what your hair's history allows, and how many visits it might take. We do the patch test at the same time.

## Treatments

### Deep conditioning treatment
A moisturising mask under gentle heat for dry or damaged hair. It can be added to any cut or colour.

### Keratin smoothing treatment
Reduces frizz and makes hair smoother and easier to blow-dry for about three to five months. Hair must not be washed for 48 hours afterwards. We use a formaldehyde-free formula. Not recommended during pregnancy.

## Special occasions

### Updo
An elegant pinned or braided style for an event. Please come with clean, dry hair, washed the day before.

### Bridal hair trial
A run-through of the wedding look a few weeks before the day, so the bride can try one or two styles and take photos.

### Wedding day styling
Our stylists come to the venue on the day for the bride and the wedding party. It is quoted per wedding after the trial, depending on the number of people and the travel.
