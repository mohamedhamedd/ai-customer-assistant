Lakeside Dental Clinic — Patient Policies

Effective 1 September 2026. These policies apply to all patients of Lakeside Dental Clinic, 420 Lakeshore Drive, Suite 200, Lake Oswego, OR 97034.

1. Appointments and cancellations

Appointment times are reserved for you. If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give us at least 24 hours' notice by phone or email so that we can offer the time to another patient.

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, and missed appointments, are subject to a late cancellation fee of $50. For appointments longer than 60 minutes, such as crowns, root canals and in-office whitening, the fee is $100. The fee is waived for a documented emergency or illness, at the clinic's discretion.

After three missed appointments without notice in a 12-month period, we may ask for a deposit before booking further appointments.

2. Late arrival

If you arrive more than 15 minutes after your appointment time, we may need to shorten your treatment or reschedule it, so that other patients are not delayed. Late arrivals are not charged a fee, but a rescheduled visit may be subject to the usual waiting time.

3. Consultation and appointment requests made online

Requests made through our website assistant, our online form or by email are requests only. An appointment is confirmed only when a member of our front desk team contacts you to confirm the date and time, usually within one working day. If you have not heard from us within two working days, please call the clinic.

4. Payment

Payment is due at the time of treatment unless a payment plan has been agreed in advance. Where insurance applies, we collect your estimated share at the visit and bill your insurer for the remainder. If your insurer pays less than estimated, we will invoice you for the difference; if it pays more, we refund the difference within 30 days.

5. Children and accompanying adults

Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, who must remain in the clinic during the visit. A parent or guardian must sign consent for treatment of a minor.

6. Privacy

We protect your health information in line with HIPAA. Your records are shared only with people involved in your care, with your insurer for billing, or where the law requires it. You can request a copy of your records at any time; we provide them within 30 days. Our full Notice of Privacy Practices is available at the front desk.

Conversations with the assistant on our website are stored so that our team can follow up on your request. Please do not share detailed medical history or payment card details through the website assistant; our team will collect these securely at your visit.

7. Online assistant and advice

The assistant on our website provides general information about our services, prices, hours and policies. It does not diagnose conditions or give medical advice. If you have symptoms, please book a consultation or, in an emergency, call us or your local emergency services.

8. Conduct

We are committed to a safe and respectful environment for patients and staff. Abusive or threatening behaviour may result in discharge from the practice.

9. Changes to these policies

We may update these policies from time to time. The current version is always available at the front desk and on our website.
