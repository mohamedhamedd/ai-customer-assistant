# Lakeside Dental Clinic — Services

Lakeside Dental Clinic is a family dental practice in Lake Oswego, Oregon. Our team, led by Dr. Elena Marsh and Dr. Samuel Okafor, provides general, cosmetic and orthodontic care for patients of all ages. Prices for every service are listed in our Pricing guide.

## General dentistry

### Check-ups and cleanings
A routine visit includes a professional cleaning by a hygienist, a dental exam, and a review of your oral health. We recommend a check-up and cleaning every six months. A routine visit takes about 45 minutes.

### New patient exam
Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, a full set of digital X-rays, and time to discuss any concerns or goals. Please allow about 60 minutes.

### Fillings
We use tooth-coloured composite fillings to repair cavities and small chips. Most fillings are completed in a single visit of 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the size and number of fillings.

### Crowns
A crown covers and protects a damaged or heavily filled tooth. We take a digital scan and fit the crown over two visits, usually two weeks apart.

### Extractions
We perform simple extractions in-office, including some wisdom teeth. Complex surgical extractions are referred to a trusted oral surgeon.

### Root canal treatment
Root canal treatment saves a tooth whose nerve is infected or inflamed. Treatment is usually completed in one or two visits of about 90 minutes each.

## Cosmetic dentistry

### Teeth whitening
We offer two whitening options:

- **In-office whitening**: a single session of about 60 to 90 minutes, performed by our hygienist with a professional-strength gel. Most patients see results of several shades lighter immediately after the session.
- **Take-home whitening kit**: custom-fitted trays and professional gel to use at home for 30 minutes a day over about two weeks.

Whitening is suitable for most adults. Some patients experience mild, temporary tooth sensitivity for a day or two afterwards. Whitening does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings. We recommend a cleaning before whitening for the best result.

### Veneers
Porcelain veneers are thin shells bonded to the front of teeth to improve shape, colour or alignment. Veneers are placed over two visits.

### Bonding
Composite bonding repairs small chips, gaps and discolouration in a single visit, usually 30 to 60 minutes per tooth.

## Orthodontics

### Invisalign
Invisalign straightens teeth with a series of clear, removable aligners. Treatment typically takes 6 to 18 months depending on the case. It starts with a free 20-minute consultation and a digital scan.

### Traditional braces
We offer metal and ceramic braces for children, teens and adults. Treatment usually takes 18 to 24 months with adjustment visits every 6 to 8 weeks.

## Dental implants
Implants replace missing teeth with a titanium post and a crown. Implant treatment is planned individually after a consultation and a 3D scan, and pricing is quoted after the consultation.

## Children's dentistry
We see children from their first birthday. Children's visits include gentle cleanings, fluoride treatment, sealants, and advice on habits and diet. Children under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

## Emergency care
We keep same-day emergency slots for severe toothache, a knocked-out or broken tooth, lost fillings or crowns, and swelling. Call us as early in the day as possible. Outside opening hours, call our main number and follow the voicemail instructions.

## Sedation and comfort
We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for anxious patients at no extra charge for most procedures. We do not offer IV sedation or general anaesthesia; patients who need it are referred to a specialist.
