# Lakeside Dental Clinic — Frequently Asked Questions

## Appointments

### Do I need an appointment?
Yes, we see patients by appointment. You can request an appointment by phone, by email, or through the assistant on our website. Requests made through the website are confirmed by our front desk, usually within one working day.

### Do you accept new patients?
Yes, we are accepting new patients of all ages.

### What should I bring to my first visit?
Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, a list of any medications you take, and any recent dental X-rays from a previous dentist. Arriving 10 minutes early helps us complete your paperwork.

### What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
Please give us at least 24 hours' notice. Our full appointment policy, including late cancellation fees, is in our Patient Policies document.

### Can I get a same-day appointment?
We keep a few same-day slots for emergencies. For routine visits, the typical wait for the next available appointment is one to two weeks.

## Treatment

### How often should I have a cleaning?
Most patients should have a check-up and cleaning every six months. Patients with gum disease may need visits every three to four months.

### Is teeth whitening safe?
Yes, professional whitening is safe for most adults when supervised by a dental professional. Some people have mild sensitivity for a day or two. Whitening is not recommended during pregnancy, for children under 16, or for teeth with untreated decay. Details of our whitening options are in our Services guide.

### Do you treat children?
Yes, we see children from their first birthday. Children under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

### I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?
Many of our patients feel the same way. Tell us when you book and we will allow extra time. We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) at no extra charge for most procedures, and you can ask for a break at any time.

### Do you offer sedation?
We offer nitrous oxide. We do not offer IV sedation or general anaesthesia; patients who need them are referred to a specialist.

### Do you do dental implants?
Yes. Implant treatment is planned after a consultation and a 3D scan, and the price is quoted at that consultation.

### Does Invisalign hurt?
Most patients feel mild pressure for a day or two after switching to a new aligner. It is not usually painful.

## Costs and insurance

### Do you accept my insurance?
We are in-network with several major PPO plans and accept most others out of network. The full list and what we do not accept are in our Insurance and Payment guide.

### What if I don't have insurance?
You can pay our standard fees directly, or join our Lakeside Smile Plan membership, which covers routine care and gives a discount on other treatment. See our Insurance and Payment guide.

### Can I pay in instalments?
Yes, for larger treatments we offer an in-house plan for orthodontics and CareCredit financing. See our Insurance and Payment guide.

## The clinic

### Where are you and is there parking?
We are at 420 Lakeshore Drive in Lake Oswego, with free parking behind the building. See our Location and Contact guide for directions and bus routes.

### What languages do you speak?
Our team speaks English and Spanish. Dr. Okafor also speaks French.

### Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?
Yes. The building has an elevator and step-free access, and we have accessible parking and restrooms.

### What do I do in a dental emergency after hours?
Call our main number and follow the voicemail instructions to reach the on-call dentist. If you have severe swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, or an injury with heavy bleeding, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.
