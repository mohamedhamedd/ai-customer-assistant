# Lakeside Dental Clinic — Insurance and Payment

## Dental insurance

We accept most PPO dental insurance plans and are in-network providers for:

- Delta Dental
- MetLife
- Cigna
- Aetna
- Guardian
- United Concordia

If your plan is not on this list, we can still see you as an out-of-network provider. We file the claim on your behalf and you pay the difference between our fee and what your plan covers.

We do not currently accept Medicaid or state dental plans, and we do not accept HMO or DMO dental plans.

Please bring your insurance card to your first visit, or send a photo of the front and back to our email address before your appointment, so we can verify your benefits in advance. Most plans cover two cleanings per year in full; coverage for other treatment varies by plan, and we will give you an estimate of your share before treatment starts.

## Payment options

We accept:

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover
- Debit cards
- Cash
- Health savings account (HSA) and flexible spending account (FSA) cards
- CareCredit financing

Payment is due at the time of treatment unless a payment plan has been arranged in advance. We do not accept personal checks.

## Payment plans and financing

For treatment over $500, such as crowns, Invisalign or braces, we offer:

- An in-house plan for orthodontic treatment: a down payment at the start of treatment and equal monthly payments over the length of treatment, with no interest.
- CareCredit, a third-party healthcare credit card with 6, 12 and 18-month interest-free options, subject to approval. You can apply online before your visit or at the front desk.

## Lakeside Smile Plan (membership for uninsured patients)

The Lakeside Smile Plan is our in-house membership for patients without dental insurance. For a monthly fee, members receive:

- Two routine cleanings and exams per year
- One set of X-rays per year
- One emergency exam per year
- 15% off all other treatment, including cosmetic and orthodontic treatment

There is no waiting period, no annual maximum and no claim forms. The plan is for individuals; family pricing is available on request. The monthly fee is listed in our Pricing guide.
