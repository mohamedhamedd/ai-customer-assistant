# Lakeside Dental Clinic — Pricing Guide

All prices are in US dollars and are the full fee before any insurance coverage. If you have insurance, your out-of-pocket cost is usually lower; see our Insurance and Payment guide. Prices were last updated in September 2026 and may change; we always confirm the cost before treatment begins.

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Routine check-up and cleaning | $129 |
| New patient exam with full X-rays | $189 |
| Emergency exam (same-day) | $95 |
| Child check-up and cleaning (under 13) | $89 |
| Fluoride treatment | $35 |
| Sealants (per tooth) | $45 |

## Orthodontics

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Invisalign or orthodontic consultation (20 minutes) | Free |
| Invisalign (full treatment) | $3,500 to $5,500 |
| Traditional braces (full treatment) | $4,000 to $6,000 |
| Retainers (per set) | $275 |

Orthodontic treatment can be paid monthly over the course of treatment; see our Insurance and Payment guide.

## Dental implants
Implant pricing depends on the number of implants and whether bone grafting is needed. A personalised quote is provided after a consultation and a 3D scan.

## Membership plan
For patients without insurance, our Lakeside Smile Plan costs $29 per month and includes two cleanings and exams per year, one set of X-rays, and 15% off all other treatment. Details are in the Insurance and Payment guide.
