# Lakeside Dental Clinic — Location and Contact

## Contact

- Phone: (503) 555-0142
- Email: hello@lakesidedental.example
- Website: www.lakesidedental.example

Our phone line is answered during opening hours. Outside those hours you can leave a voicemail, and we return calls on the next working day. For a dental emergency outside opening hours, call the main number and follow the voicemail instructions to reach the on-call dentist.

## Address

Lakeside Dental Clinic
420 Lakeshore Drive, Suite 200
Lake Oswego, OR 97034

We are on the second floor of the Lakeshore Professional Building, above the pharmacy. The building has an elevator and the clinic is fully wheelchair accessible.

## Parking and transport

- Free patient parking is available in the lot behind the building; enter from Lakeshore Drive. Validation is not required.
- Two accessible parking spaces are next to the rear entrance.
- TriMet bus lines 35 and 36 stop at Lakeshore Drive and A Avenue, a two-minute walk from the clinic.
- Bicycle racks are at the front entrance.
