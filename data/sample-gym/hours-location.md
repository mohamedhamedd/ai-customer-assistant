# Acme Fitness — Opening Hours and Location

## Opening hours (staffed)

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 5:30 AM to 10:00 PM |
| Saturday | 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM |
| Sunday | 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM |

Premium members can use the gym floor 24/7 with their key fob; outside staffed hours, the studios, sauna and front desk are closed.

On public holidays the gym is open 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, with no group classes.

## Address and contact

Acme Fitness, 410 Market Street, Riverside.
Phone: (312) 555-0187
Email: hello@acme-fitness.example

The front desk answers the phone during staffed hours.

## Parking and transport

Members park free for up to 2 hours in the Market Street garage next door; get your ticket stamped at the front desk. The Riverside Central bus stop is a 3-minute walk away. There is a bike rack at the entrance.
