# Acme Fitness — Memberships and Pricing

All prices are in US dollars and include tax. Prices were last updated in September 2026. Memberships renew automatically each month until cancelled (see Policies in the FAQ).

## Membership plans

| Plan | Price | What it includes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | $39 per month | Gym floor and cardio area during staffed hours |
| Plus | $59 per month | Everything in Basic, plus unlimited group classes and the sauna |
| Premium | $89 per month | Everything in Plus, plus one personal training session a month and 24/7 access |
| Student (with a valid student ID) | $29 per month | Same as Basic |

There is a one-time joining fee of $25. The joining fee is waived when you join during your free trial week.

## Day passes and class packs

## Free trial

Every new member can try the gym free for 7 days, including group classes. You need to show a photo ID at the front desk; no card details are needed for the trial.
