# Acme Fitness — Classes and Training

## Group classes

Group classes are included in the Plus and Premium plans and can also be booked with a day pass or a class pack. Classes last 45 minutes unless noted. Book a spot in the members app up to 7 days ahead; each class has 20 spots.

### Weekly class timetable

| Class | Days | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Spin | Monday, Wednesday, Friday | 6:30 AM and 6:00 PM |
| Yoga (all levels, 60 minutes) | Tuesday and Thursday | 7:00 PM |
| HIIT | Monday and Thursday | 12:15 PM |
| Strength basics | Tuesday and Saturday | 9:00 AM |
| Pilates | Wednesday | 7:00 PM |
| Family yoga (ages 8 and up with an adult) | Sunday | 10:00 AM |

There are no group classes on public holidays.

### What to bring

Bring a towel and a water bottle. Yoga and Pilates mats are provided. For spin, regular trainers work; cycling shoes with SPD cleats can also be used.

## Personal training

Our certified personal trainers build a plan around your goals, whether that's getting started, getting stronger or training for an event. Sessions are booked directly with a trainer at the front desk or in the members app. Prices are in the Memberships and Pricing guide.

New members get a free 45-minute introductory session: a tour, a movement check and a starter plan.

## Facilities

- Gym floor with free weights, racks and machines
- Cardio area with treadmills, rowers and bikes
- Two studios for group classes
- Sauna (Plus and Premium plans)
- Changing rooms with showers and day lockers (bring your own padlock)

There is no swimming pool.
