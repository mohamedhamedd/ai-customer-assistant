# Acme Fitness — Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I join?

Join at the front desk or in the members app. You need a photo ID and a debit or credit card. Plans and prices are in the Memberships and Pricing guide.

## Can I try the gym before joining?

Yes. New members can try it free for 7 days, including classes. See Free trial in the Memberships and Pricing guide.

## How do I cancel my membership?

Cancel at the front desk or by emailing us, with 30 days' notice. There is no cancellation fee and no minimum contract after the first month.

## Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, for 1 to 3 months a year, for medical reasons or travel. A frozen membership costs $10 per month.

## Can I bring a guest?

Plus and Premium members can bring one guest per visit, up to 4 times a month, free. Guests must be 16 or older and show a photo ID.

## What is the minimum age?

Members must be 16 or older. Members aged 14 and 15 can join with a parent or guardian's signature and must train with an adult. Family yoga on Sundays is open to children from 8 with an adult.

## Do you offer nutrition plans?

Our trainers give general tips during personal training sessions. We don't offer meal plans or nutrition consultations.

## I have an injury or a health condition. Can I still train?

Talk to your doctor first, then tell your trainer so they can adjust your plan. Our trainers don't give medical advice.
